Headcount in birds, not vibes

Broiler, layer, or kienyeji — by batch, by house.

Birds run as flocks: day-old, live count, day-to-market, and medicine windows. FarmTrack tracks the batch, the house, the feed, and the mortality with timestamps — not with “about three hundred, give or take.”

Flock

A building isn’t a spreadsheet row

You already think in “Coop 3, broiler, week four.” The software just refuses to let that stay in your head. Medication schedules, withdrawal, and the next dose are visible to the people who need them — the rest of the farm doesn’t get noise it won’t use.

You don’t need a different app for “layers” — you need a different set of questions on the same honest grid.

What leaving looks like

Sale, cull, natural loss — each exit is named. Egg trays and broiler live weight can sit beside the same temperature notes if you want them, without inventing a second ledger for “production.”
