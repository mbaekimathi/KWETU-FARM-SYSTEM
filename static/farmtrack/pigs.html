Every pig, one thread from farrow to truck.

Register animals with a real ID, record litters with honesty, and link feed to the sack it left. Your crew logs on phones; the board still sees a straight line to profit or loss.

Narrative

Pigs are individuals until they aren’t

Sows, boars, finishers, and the occasional batch: FarmTrack names the stage, not the story you wish had happened. Breeding, farrowing, weaning, and exit each carry their own line — and when two workers disagree, the audit line wins.

“I thought we had eleven alive.” — No. You have eleven, two stillborn, and a weight sheet.

What you stop improvising

Vaccine windows by programme, not by which calendar page got wet. Feed issues matched to the bin that was opened. A growth curve that doesn’t need to be “explained” in a meeting. Purple handles the form; the orange note on the day sheet is the animal — we keep the two in separate colors on purpose, never blended.
